## Asset readiness

- Each matched calculator publishes its formula and method-review date in the interface.
- Wage and payout tools state that they do not calculate withholding, taxes, benefits, classification, or jurisdiction-specific rules.
- The proration tool states its equal-daily-accrual assumption and directs users to confirm contract and closing-professional figures.
- Automated representative cases cover overtime, nonnegative payouts, leap years, and closing-day conventions.
- The Field Day Planner publishes its capacity formula, keeps travel only between stops, and labels traffic and route-optimization limits.
- The Repair Cost Worksheet starts at zero, publishes its quantity × unit-cost and contingency method, and clearly separates a budget from bids, appraisal adjustments, inspections, and code opinions.
- The Paired Sales Support worksheet publishes the extraction formula, excludes rows without a feature difference, shows the full range and median, and warns that pairing and reconciliation require appraisal judgment.
- The Seller Net Sheet and Break-Even Billing Rate both start with zero-dollar assumptions, save locally, publish their formulas, and distinguish planning estimates from professional closing, tax, legal, accounting, or market advice.
- The Property Inspection Intake sheet is printable, starts blank, saves only on the device, and clearly separates owner/occupant statements from observed and verified appraisal data.
- The Rental Property Snapshot publishes its vacancy, NOI, cap-rate, GRM, DSCR, and cash-on-cash formulas and warns users to verify the rent roll, expenses, reserves, financing, and local requirements.
- The Cash Reserve Runway publishes its reserve-floor and monthly-burn method, includes a month-by-month table, and labels the repeated-average assumption and professional limitations.
